Our project is an idea for a restaurant located inside our college. The main goal is to provide students, professors, and other college staff with a comfortable and convenient place to enjoy delicious food during their breaks. The restaurant will offer a variety of meals, including grilled dishes, salads, soups, pasta, rice, desserts, and drinks, to suit different tastes. We designed a website for the restaurant to help customers explore the menu and learn more about the available food options. This project combines creativity and technology to present a simple and practical idea that could improve the college experience.
