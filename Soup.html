Soup

  • Molokhia

    Egypt's most famous green soup, made from finely minced jute mallow leaves simmered in a rich chicken, rabbit, or lamb broth. It is finished with tasha—a sizzling garlic and coriander paste fried in ghee—and typically served over white rice or with flatbread

  • Lisan Asfoor

    A classic Egyptian comfort food and go-to remedy when feeling sick. It consists of tiny, grain-shaped lisan asfoor (orzo) pasta toasted golden in butter or oil, then simmered in a flavorful chicken or beef broth seasoned with mastic and bay leaves.

  • Shorbat Ads

    A thick, warming winter staple made from red lentils, carrots, onions, tomatoes, and potatoes, blended smooth and spiced with cumin and turmeric. It is often served with a squeeze of fresh lemon juice, toasted pita chips, or a side of vermicelli rice.

  • Chorba / Tomato & Vegetable Soups

    Lighter or spiced broth-based soups incorporating local seasonal vegetables, sometimes adapted with regional Mediterranean or North African flavor profiles
