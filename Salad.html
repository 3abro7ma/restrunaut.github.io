Salade

  • Salata Baladi

    Egypt’s national green salad made with finely diced tomatoes, cucumbers, onions, green peppers, and fresh parsley, tossed with lemon juice, oil, and dried mint. The juice that collects at the bottom of the bowl is famously known as Salata Whiskey and is often enjoyed on its own.

  • Yogurt Salad

    Cool yogurt mixed with finely grated cucumber, garlic, and dried mint.

  • Mish Salad

    an Egyptian cheese dish made from pungent fermented mish cheese, or a classic "mish-mash" chopped deli salad
