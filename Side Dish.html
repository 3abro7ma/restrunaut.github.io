Side dish

  • Tahina

    A silky, sesame-seed paste condiment seasoned with lemon juice, garlic, and cumin, served alongside almost every grilled meat or fish.

  • Saneyet Batates

    A comforting potato bake layered with sliced onions, garlic, and rich tomato sauce, often baked in the oven.

  • Mesaka'a

    Fried eggplant and sweet peppers baked in a garlicky, spiced tomato sauce (sometimes layered with minced meat).

  • Bamya

    Young okra cooked in a savory tomato and garlic broth, served either as a side or a full meat stew.

  • mahshi mix

    Vegetables like zucchini, eggplants, or bell peppers stuffed with spiced herb rice.
